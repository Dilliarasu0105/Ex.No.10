## Date:06/09/2026
## Reg. No.212223230049

## Aim:
To demonstrate how various prompting techniques (query decomposition, decision-making, semantic filtering, etc.) can be employed to create content such as reports, articles, case studies, or creative works like comic books, using ChatGPT or similar models. The objective is to highlight how different prompt structures affect the content's quality, coherence, and structure.

## Procedure:
1.	Introduction to Prompt Patterns: Begin by understanding the following prompt patterns:
○	Query Decomposition: Breaking down complex queries into smaller, actionable parts.
○	Decision Making: Asking the model to choose between options or directions.
○	Answer Engineering: Refining outputs by giving detailed instructions on how to structure or format the answer.
○	Fact Check List: Ensuring the content is factually accurate, especially for reports or case studies.
○	Tail Generation: Extending the content logically to create depth in storytelling or analysis.
○	Menu Actions: Presenting multiple action choices and guiding the AI to select one.
○	Semantic Filter: Applying filters to control the tone, style, and accuracy of the content.
2.	Choosing the Content Type: Decide on the type of content you want to create. This could be:
○	Reports (e.g., industry analysis, sustainability reports, etc.)
○	Case Studies (e.g., business solutions, technological innovations)
○	Articles (e.g., opinion pieces, educational articles)
○	Creative Content (e.g., comic book story, short stories, video scripts)
3.	For example, you could choose a business report on market trends, a case study on a successful startup, or even a story-based prompt like creating a fictional world for a comic.
4.	Creating the Prompts:
○	Start with simple prompts to generate initial content.
○	Gradually refine the prompts, moving toward more complex techniques like decision-making (asking the model to pick one of several options), tail generation (extending the narrative), and semantic filtering (adjusting style or tone).
5.	Generating and Refining Outputs: Use the model to generate initial drafts, and then refine the outputs using iterative adjustments:
○	For a report, prompt the model with basic data, then ask for a deeper analysis and insights.
○	For creative content, refine the initial story idea with specific details on characters, settings, or actions.
6.	Review and Evaluation: After generating content, evaluate the outputs for:
○	Coherence: Is the generated content logical and structured?
○	Creativity/Originality: Is the content engaging and fresh?
○	Accuracy: Is the content factually accurate (for reports and case studies)?
○	Tone and Style: Does the content match the intended tone (formal, creative, educational)?

# TEST CASE 1: ARTICLE ON CLIMATE CHANGE

## 1. Basic Prompt

> Write an article about climate change.

---

## 2. First Draft

# Climate Change

Climate change refers to long-term changes in the Earth's temperature and weather patterns. Human activities such as burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrial development contribute to the increase of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere.

The effects of climate change include rising temperatures, melting glaciers, rising sea levels, droughts, floods, and changes in weather patterns. These changes can affect humans, animals, plants, and ecosystems.

Climate change can be reduced by using renewable energy, planting trees, reducing pollution, conserving energy, and promoting sustainable practices.

---

## 3. Refined Prompt

> Write a well-structured educational article on climate change. Use query decomposition to cover the following sections: Introduction, Causes, Effects, Solutions, and Conclusion. Use a formal and informative tone. Keep the content clear and easy for students to understand. Include suitable headings and concise paragraphs.

---

## 4. Refined Content

# Climate Change: Causes, Effects and Solutions

## Introduction

Climate change refers to long-term changes in the Earth's climate, including changes in temperature, rainfall, and weather patterns. Human activities have significantly contributed to recent climate changes by increasing the concentration of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere.

## Causes of Climate Change

One of the major causes of climate change is the burning of fossil fuels such as coal, oil, and natural gas. These activities release greenhouse gases, including carbon dioxide, into the atmosphere.

Deforestation is another important cause. Trees absorb carbon dioxide, and cutting down forests reduces the Earth's ability to absorb these gases.

Industrial activities, transportation, and certain agricultural practices also contribute to greenhouse gas emissions.

## Effects of Climate Change

Climate change can lead to rising global temperatures, melting glaciers, and rising sea levels. It can also increase the frequency or intensity of some extreme weather events.

Changes in climate can affect agriculture, water availability, ecosystems, and biodiversity. Coastal regions may also face risks due to rising sea levels.

## Solutions

Climate change can be addressed by reducing greenhouse gas emissions and adopting sustainable practices. Renewable energy sources such as solar and wind power can reduce dependence on fossil fuels.

Energy conservation, public transportation, forest protection, and responsible consumption can also help reduce environmental impact.

## Conclusion

Climate change is a major environmental challenge that requires action from individuals, industries, and governments. By reducing pollution and adopting sustainable practices, society can work towards a cleaner and more sustainable future.

---

## 5. Observation

The basic prompt generated a simple overview of climate change. The refined prompt produced more organized content with clear headings, better structure, and detailed explanations.

Query decomposition helped divide the topic into smaller sections, while answer engineering improved the format and readability of the article.

# TEST CASE 2: ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IMPACT ON HEALTHCARE

## 1. Basic Prompt

> Write about the impact of Artificial Intelligence on healthcare.

---

## 2. First Draft

# Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

Artificial Intelligence is becoming increasingly useful in healthcare. AI systems can analyze medical data, assist healthcare professionals, and support decision-making.

AI can help identify patterns in medical images and large datasets. It can also support patient monitoring and improve the efficiency of certain healthcare processes.

However, AI systems also require reliable data, proper testing, privacy protection, and human oversight.

---

## 3. Refined Prompt

> Create an educational report on the impact of Artificial Intelligence in healthcare. Use query decomposition and organize the content into Introduction, Applications, Benefits, Challenges, and Conclusion. Use a formal and informative tone suitable for engineering students. Clearly explain both the positive impact and the challenges of using AI in healthcare.

---

## 4. Refined Content

# Impact of Artificial Intelligence on Healthcare

## Introduction

Artificial Intelligence is increasingly being used to support healthcare services and medical research. AI systems can process large amounts of data and identify patterns that may help healthcare professionals in analysis and decision-making.

## Applications of AI in Healthcare

AI can be used in areas such as medical image analysis, patient monitoring, data analysis, and healthcare administration.

Machine learning systems can assist in identifying patterns in medical images and other healthcare data. AI-based systems may also support the analysis of large volumes of medical information.

## Benefits of AI

One important benefit of AI is its ability to process large amounts of information efficiently. AI tools can assist healthcare professionals by providing data-driven insights and supporting routine tasks.

AI can also contribute to research by helping scientists analyze complex datasets.

## Challenges

The use of AI in healthcare involves several challenges. Medical data must be protected to maintain privacy and security.

AI systems can also produce inaccurate or biased results if they are trained on poor-quality or unrepresentative data. Therefore, AI systems should be properly tested and used with appropriate human oversight.

## Conclusion

Artificial Intelligence has the potential to support and improve several areas of healthcare. However, successful implementation requires reliable data, responsible system design, privacy protection, testing, and human supervision.

AI should be viewed as a tool that can assist healthcare professionals rather than completely replace human expertise.

---

## 5. Observation

The basic prompt generated a general explanation of Artificial Intelligence in healthcare. The refined prompt generated a more structured and detailed report.

The use of prompt patterns improved the organization, clarity, and depth of the generated content. Query decomposition helped separate the topic into important sections, while semantic filtering ensured that the content maintained a formal and educational tone.
